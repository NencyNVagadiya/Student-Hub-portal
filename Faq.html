• How can I register for an event? You can register for an event by clicking on the "Registration Now" button next to the event you want to participate in. This will take you to the registration page where you can fill out your details and submit your registration.

• Is event registration free? YES

• Where will the event be conducted? ON 30 -4-26

• Can I participate in more than one event? Yes, you can register for multiple events. Simply click on the "Registration Now" button for each event you wish to participate in and complete the registration process for each one.
